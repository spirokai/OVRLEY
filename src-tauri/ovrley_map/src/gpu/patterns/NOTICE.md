# Bundled fill patterns

Raw RGBA8 crops of `wetland_bg_11` (30 x 30) and `pedestrian_polygon` (128 x 128) from OpenFreeMap's
`https://tiles.openfreemap.org/sprites/ofm_f384/ofm@2x.png`, the sprite every preset style names.

They belong to [OpenFreeMap styles](https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles), which is MIT
licensed. The Liberty style forks [OSM Liberty](https://github.com/maputnik/osm-liberty) and derives
from OSM Bright and Mapbox Open Styles, whose design is released under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). See that project's `LICENSE.md`.
