FFmpeg
------
This distribution includes unmodified FFmpeg and FFprobe command-line binaries as separate
components. OVRLEY runs them as subprocesses (ffmpeg to encode video, ffprobe to read video
metadata); they are not linked into the OVRLEY executable. The exact build and its license follow at
the end of this file.

Project: https://ffmpeg.org/
Source code: https://ffmpeg.org/download.html
License information: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
Windows and Linux builds: BtbN FFmpeg builds, https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
macOS builds: https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/
